05 · Data Governance & Audit

Research Methodology & Transparency

Every metric documents its reporting period, currency normalization basis, source link, and explicit confidence tier.

Ranking & Share-Class Rules

  • Ranked by consolidated USD equity market capitalization across primary and secondary share classes.
  • Dual-class issuers (Alphabet GOOGL/GOOG, Berkshire Hathaway A/B, Samsung common/pref) appear once.
  • Private enterprises (e.g., SpaceX, ByteDance) are excluded; only exchange-listed equities are tracked.

Confidence Classification

  • VERIFIED_PRIMARY Statutory annual reports (10-K, 20-F, IFRS filings) and IR releases.
  • VERIFIED_SECONDARY Cross-checked market aggregators & financial databases.
  • ESTIMATED Reference snapshot valuations, FX conversions, and modeled float/liquidity splits.

Derived Financial Formulas

  • Net Worth Check: Reported Equity (cross-checked via Total Assets − Total Liabilities).
  • Margins: Gross / Operating / Net Income ÷ Revenue × 100.
  • Leverage: Liabilities ÷ Assets × 100 and Equity ÷ Assets × 100.
  • Free Cash Flow: Operating Cash Flow − Capital Expenditure.
  • Valuation Multiple: Market Cap ÷ Annual Revenue.
